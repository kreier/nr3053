Hardware Specifications & Teardown

Manufactured by Shenzhen SDMC Technology Co., Ltd. for Viettel's Home WiFi 6 lineup, the SDMC NR3053 packs remarkably generous hardware for a budget carrier router: a MediaTek MT7981BA (Filogic 820) SoC, 512 MiB DDR3L, 256 MiB SPI NAND, an integrated-FEM MT7976CN radio, and a clearly silkscreened 3.3 V UART header.

Inside the Enclosure: Heatsink, Antennas & UART

Removing the bottom screws and prying open the white plastic casing reveals the blue WIFI6-MT-02.C mainboard. A large black-anodized aluminum heatsink (retained by four plastic push-pins) covers the SoC, RAM, switch, and Wi-Fi radio.

  • 5-Lead RF Design: Three coaxial paths (CONG3, CONG4, CONG5) serve the 5 GHz band (2T3R/3T3R receive diversity for stronger client return signals), while two paths (CONG1, CONG2) serve 2.4 GHz (2×2 MIMO).
  • Developer-Friendly UART: The 4-pin header at the top-left corner has clear silkscreen labels (GND, TX, RX, VCC), making serial console access and mtk_uartboot unbricking straightforward without probing hidden test pads.
  • Unpopulated Right-Side Pads: The right side of the PCB contains unpopulated footprints where hardware modders can add an optional USB port (requiring a USB-enabled ImmortalWrt build).

Core Semiconductors (Heatsink & Shield Removed)

Lifting the aluminum heatsink and opening the metal EMI shield frame exposes every major IC on the top of the board:

  • MediaTek MT7981BA (Filogic 820): First-class mainline Linux and OpenWrt/ImmortalWrt support under the mediatek/filogic target, with open-source mt76 wireless drivers and hardware packet acceleration (HNAT / WED).
  • Linkage 4GF4MLF-GJS (512 MiB DDR3L): Double the memory of most budget AX3000 ISP routers, providing comfortable headroom for AdGuard Home, SQM/cake, WireGuard, or Tailscale.
  • MediaTek MT7531AE: Dedicated Gigabit Ethernet switch connected to the SoC via a 2.5 Gbps internal link (2500base-x), routing all 4 physical RJ45 ports (wan, lan1, lan2, lan3).
  • MediaTek MT7976CN: Companion Wi-Fi 6 RF transceiver supporting 1024-QAM and 160 MHz channel width on 5 GHz. Front-end modules (FEMs) are integrated directly inside the IC package.

Rear PCB & 256 MiB SPI NAND Flash

Flipping the PCB reveals a clean underside with large copper ground pours for passive cooling and the single non-volatile storage chip:

Dosilicon DS35Q2GBS Bootloader Compatibility
The NR3053 uses a Dosilicon DS35Q2GBS 256 MiB SPI NAND chip. Never flash a BL2 preloader built for a different router or an old release that lacks DS35Q2GBS support — doing so will prevent booting from NAND and require mtk_uartboot recovery over UART.
