Verified Stock Viettel Partition Table (mtd0–mtd11)

Decoded directly from live NR3053 hardware and SHA-256-verified MTD dumps (stock OpenWrt 21.02-SNAPSHOT r0-855a6718f / Linux 5.4.194 aarch64, U-Boot 2022.07-rc3 (Sep 20 2025), NAND geometry 2048 B page / 128 KiB erase block / 128 B OOB / 8-bit ECC / 0 bad blocks). mtd0 ("spi0.0") exposes the full raw 256.00 MiB chip, mtd1–mtd10 cover exactly 240.00 MiB (0x00000000–0x0f000000), and mtd11 ("reserved") reports 0 B while MediaTek NMBM reserves the upper 16.00 MiB tail.

Stock UBI Containers, Redundant Pairs & u-boot-env

  • 4 Stock UBI Containers (124 KiB LEB): ubi0 on mtd5 (kernel 6.0 MiB + rootfs 20.1 MiB, SquashFS /rom 13.0 MiB); ubi1 on mtd6 (identical mirror); ubi2 on mtd7 (rootfs_data 128.1 MiB UBIFS, mounted /overlay 116.6 MiB usable); ubi3 on mtd8 (rootfs_data_backup 18.0 MiB).
  • Verified SHA-256 Identical Backup Pairs: mtd3 (Factory) ≡ mtd9 (Factory_backup), mtd4 (FIP) ≡ mtd10 (fip2), and mtd5 (firmware) ≡ mtd6 (firmware2). Only mtd7 (rootfs_data) and mtd8 (rootfs_data_backup) differ.
  • Decoded u-boot-env (mtd2, CRC32 valid): Contains upgrade_result=0 and bootimage=2 (dual-bank firmware slot selector).

Why Stock MTD Numbers Shift by One

On the stock Viettel firmware, mtd0 exposes the entire raw 256 MiB chip ("spi0.0"), so individual partitions start at mtd1. Under ImmortalWrt, BL2 is mtd0. The physical NAND offsets of the first 6 MiB are identical:

Physical NAND Offset Stock Viettel ROM ImmortalWrt 25.12 Migration Note
0x000000–0x100000BL2 (mtd1)BL2 (mtd0)Keep stock BL2 unless specifically needed (high brick risk).
0x100000–0x200000u-boot-env (mtd2)u-boot-env (mtd1)Legacy env; ImmortalWrt stores active env inside UBI.
0x200000–0x400000Factory (mtd3)Factory (mtd2)NEVER ERASE — holds Wi-Fi RF EEPROM & MACs.
0x400000–0x600000FIP (mtd4)FIP (mtd3)Flash ImmortalWrt U-Boot FIP here (same offset 0x400000).
0x600000–0x0f000000firmware (32M) + firmware2 (32M) + rootfs_data (142M) + rootfs_data_backup (24M) + Factory_backup (2M) + fip2 (2M)ubi (234 MiB Native UBI)Incompatible OS layout — cannot run sysupgrade directly from stock; back up mtd1–mtd10 before migrating.
Single Native UBI vs. Stock 4× UBI + NMBM Warning
Stock Viettel firmware uses MediaTek's NMBM bad-block mapping layer with 4 separate UBI containers (ubi0–ubi3 across mtd5–mtd8) plus backup copies of Factory (mtd9) and fip2 (mtd10), whereas ImmortalWrt 25.12 merges 0x00600000–0x0f000000 into a single 234 MiB Native UBI container (without NMBM). Once ImmortalWrt U-Boot formats the UBI container, all stock partitions above 0x600000 (including Factory_backup and fip2) are wiped. Always back up stock MTD partitions (mtd1–mtd10, especially Factory) first.
