Viettel SDMC NR3053 — OpenWrt & ImmortalWrt Guide

Complete bilingual documentation for unlocking, flashing, and tuning the Viettel SDMC NR3053 Wi-Fi 6 (AX3000) router. Powered by the MediaTek Filogic 820 (MT7981BA) SoC with 512 MiB DDR3L RAM and 256 MiB SPI NAND flash, the NR3053 runs ImmortalWrt 25.12, OpenWrt 24.10, and community KeeneticOS builds.

Method Difficulty Requires Case Open? Best For
1. Stock Web Failsafe (No SSH) Easiest No Units with stock U-Boot 2022.07-rc3 Web Failsafe: flash OpenWrt 24.10 directly or upgrade FIP via /uboot.html.
2. Unlock SSH → ImmortalWrt 25.12 Medium No Full MTD/Factory backup before flashing, or older stock units without Web Failsafe. Gives full 234 MiB Native UBI.
3. UART + TFTP (mtk_uartboot) Advanced Yes (3.3 V TTL) Unbricking, testing initramfs-recovery.itb in RAM without touching NAND, or clearing legacy NMBM.
Always Protect the Factory Partition (0x200000)
Your router's unique Wi-Fi RF calibration EEPROM and Ethernet MAC addresses live inside the 2 MiB Factory partition. Never erase it, and back it up as soon as you gain SSH or UART access.
